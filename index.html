Sessions are fixed in New York time (Asia 20:00–00:00, London 02:00–05:00, New York 07:00–10:00, lunch 12:00–13:00) and shown in your chosen zone. Daily bars open 17:00 NY, weekly Sunday 17:00 NY. Camarilla pivots use the previous day (17:00–17:00 NY), previous week (Sun 17:00–Fri 17:00 NY) or previous calendar month. H3/L3, H4/L4 and H5/L5 are solid, H1/H2/L1/L2 dashed, P (pivot) and CP (previous close) dotted. Clock findings (from one year of data): red bands lean down, green leans up, gold = the 9–11 am window where the day's high or low is most often set. Details in gold-daily-clock.html. Trades: box from entry to exit, green = profit, red = loss, triangle = entry, dot = exit. Table times are New York. Replay: space play/pause, → step, ← back.
